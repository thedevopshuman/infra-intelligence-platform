# Security policy

The project is pre-release and does not yet accept production deployments.

Do not open a public issue containing credentials, customer data, infrastructure identifiers, prompts with sensitive context, or exploit details. Until a private reporting channel is established, record the need for one in `docs/roadmap/initial-roadmap.md` and keep sensitive findings out of the repository.

The baseline threat model and authority model are documented in [`docs/architecture/security-tenancy.md`](docs/architecture/security-tenancy.md).
